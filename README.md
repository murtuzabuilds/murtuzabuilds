### How I work

- **Start with the handoff.** Every AI feature is a decision about who does what: the model, the person, or both. I design that line first.
- **Ground everything.** Answers come from data and documents, not model memory. Every agent decision should come with a sentence explaining it.
- **Measure trust, not just accuracy.** Approval rates, override rates and time to autonomy tell you whether people will actually rely on an AI.
