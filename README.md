### I build AI products from the data layer up.

AI product manager and MBA candidate (Technology Strategy & Product Management) at the Wisconsin School of Business. Right now I'm a **Senior AI PM Intern at Lighthouse AI**, working on regulatory intelligence. Before that: about four years at **Deloitte** in product strategy and digital transformation, delivering enterprise AI, and product design at **Tinder**.

Digital transformation taught me where work actually breaks: in the manual handoffs between people, tools and data. AI product management is how I fix it now. My work sits in the shift from Human-Computer Interaction to Human-AI Interaction: deciding what the AI should do, what the person should decide, and how each one knows what the other is doing.

It finds the AI tools and agents people are using, stops sensitive data from being sent to them, gives each agent an owner and limits, and turns unapproved AI use into a plan for where to invest next. ### How I work

- **Start with the handoff.** Every AI feature is a decision about who does what: the model, the person, or both. I design that line first.
- **Ground everything.** Answers come from data and documents, not model memory. Every agent decision should come with a sentence explaining it.
- **Transform the workflow, not just the tool.** Automating a broken process only makes it break faster. Redesign the work, then add the AI.
- **Measure trust, not just accuracy.** Approval rates, override rates and time to autonomy tell you whether people will actually rely on an AI.
